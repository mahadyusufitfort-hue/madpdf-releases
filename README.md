# MadPDF: offline PDF editor for Windows (English & Arabic)

**Edit, sign, convert, OCR and protect PDFs on your own PC. Your files are never uploaded.** Built for offices where one invoice mixes English and Arabic.

## What it does

| | |
|---|---|
| **Edit text & images** | Change words, fix a number, swap or move an image right on the page, in the original font where the PDF allows it. |
| **Comments & markup** | Sticky notes, highlight, underline, strike-through, stamps and drawing tools, all in one comments panel. |
| **Fill & Sign, forms** | Fill any form, add your signature or initials, or build your own form with automatic field detection. |
| **Organize pages** | Combine (merge), split, extract, insert, rotate, reorder and delete pages in a page grid. |
| **Create PDF** | Turn Word, Excel and PowerPoint files or images into PDF. Office files need the free LibreOffice on the same PC. |
| **Export to Office** | Get a PDF back into Word, Excel or PowerPoint. |
| **Scan & OCR** | Recognise text in scans and photos, in **English and Arabic**, and save searchable PDFs. |
| **Protect & redact** | Passwords, print/copy restrictions, permanent redaction and removal of hidden information. |
| **Digital signatures** | Sign with a certificate, add a trusted timestamp, and keep signatures verifiable with long-term validation (LTV). |
| **Preflight, PDF/A, PDF/X** | Check files against archive and print standards and convert to PDF/A or PDF/X. |
| **Print production** | Output Preview with separations and ink coverage, overprint simulation and printer marks. |
| **Compare documents** | Two versions side by side, with every text change shown. |
| **Accessibility** | Every tool works from the keyboard, and documents can be tagged for screen readers. |

## English & Arabic, done properly

- The whole interface in English or Arabic, laid out right to left in Arabic.
- Arabic letters joined and ordered correctly, in English and Arabic documents alike.
- Type right to left and edit Arabic text that is already in a PDF.
- Arabic OCR turns scanned pages into searchable text.
- Word and Excel exports keep Arabic right to left.

## Download

- **[Get it from the Microsoft Store](https://getmadtools.com/madpdf/download.php?to=store&s=github)**: installs with no Windows warning and the Store keeps it up to date.
- **[Installer (latest)](https://getmadtools.com/madpdf/download.php?s=github)**, or pick a version under [Releases](https://github.com/mahadyusufitfort-hue/madpdf-releases/releases).
- Deploying to many PCs? Use the MSI from Releases: `msiexec /i MadPDF-<version>-x64.msi /qn ALLUSERS=1`
- If the installer makes Windows show "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**. That is normal for a new app from a small publisher.

MadPDF checks for new versions and offers to install them.

## Privacy

Opening, editing, OCR, signing and saving all happen on your PC. There is no account and no telemetry. MadPDF goes online only when you check for updates, sign with a trusted timestamp, check a certificate's revocation status, or choose to send feedback. See the [privacy policy](https://getmadtools.com/privacy.html).
